Disengagement due to mechanical flexibility

The height of the threads is choosen sufficiently high in order to eliminate any risk of disengagement in service conditions. The portion of the thread height which remains tight under load is calculated by calculating the radial displacement of the channel threads, and disregarding the radial displacement of the ring threads.

The radial displacement of the channel in the threaded portion is calculated by taxing into account the longitudinal moment generated by the total axial force applied to the threads by the lock ring and the internal pressure that would be installed in case of leakage through the channel gasket.
