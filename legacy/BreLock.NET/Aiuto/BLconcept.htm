Design concept of the breech-lock closure

Channel barrel threaded end

The minimum thickness of the threaded part of the channel barrel is calculated to withstand the combined effects of internal pressure (membrane longitudinal and hoop stresses) and of the longitudianl bending moment transmitted by the lock-ring.

This means that in case of leaking from channel cover gasket the maximum operating conditions will not cause deformation of the threaded channel part.

Vent in the region of gasket diaphragm compression ring is foreseen mainly for leak detection and alarm.

Threaded lock ring

The threaded lock ring is design to withstand the total load coming from:

·           Full tubeside design pressure load

·           External (channel gasket) and, if present, internal (tubesheet gasket) lock ring bolts design loads.

The lock ring threads are checked for combined bend and shear.

The adopted thread contact surface is greater than the minimum required in order to account for possible disengagement due to displacement between lock ring and channel barrel.

Displacement from design loads and thermal loads is checked. Maximum displacement is found at transient conditions.

Gasket compression ring and external bolts on the threaded lock ring.

The seating load is transmitted to the gasket by external bolts through gasket compression ring and through the external rim of the diaphragm.

External bolts are sized for tubeside full design conditions and hydrotest conditions.

The adopted mean gasket diameter and bolts circle diameter are the same. This assures in line transmission of the load to the gasket avoiding rotation of the gasket diaphragm.

The gasket diaphragm is subject to possible deformation because of cyclic thermal expansion during transient conditions and because of the differential thermal expansion coefficient compared to the cover material. In the long term the diaphragm may require replacement according to deformations allowed by maintenance manual.

In case tubesheet is fixed with push rods the following will also apply:

 

Inner ring and split ring

They are sized to differential pressure and gasket seating load at design and hydrotest conditions.

The construction solution foresees a compact split ring, easy to handle and to fit in the channel ring seat, and a free inner ring designed to transmit total loads to the split ring, directly, and to the threaded lock ring, via inner bolts and push rods.

 

Internal bolts on the inner ring

The internal bolts guarantee the initial seating of the tubesheet gasket and its tightness under hydrotest and operating condition. They are sized to differential pressure at design and hydrotest conditions.

To avoid plastic deformation at threaded region, bolts have reduced section at load transmission contact surface. Bolts section is however smaller than push rods section.

Normally the inner ring and the internal bolts are located immediately under the diaphragm. This configuration is favoured for quick inspection and cost optimisation.

As an alternative, the inner ring may also be located as close as possible to the tubesheet, thus reducing the effects of differential thermal expansion. Yet this is a more expensive configuration.

In the long term inner bolts are however subject to plastic deformation due to differential thermal expansion in the axial direction between channel and internal cage. Inspection and substitution of wear internal bolts is addressed by maintenance manual. 

Internal bolts on the lock ring

These bolts are not working in hydrotest and initial operating condition as they are available for in service re-tightening of the tubesheet joint. They are designed as a full back-up device for the internal bolts on the inner ring in service condition.
